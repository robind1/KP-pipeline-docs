KPtoFHIR
========

KPtoFHIR is a Nextflow pipeline that turns *Klebsiella pneumoniae* sequencing data or assemblies
into Kleborate typing, resistance genes and their plasmid or chromosomal location, a predicted
susceptibility panel and cgMLST, reported as HL7 FHIR R4 resources that conform to the Genomics
Reporting IG 3.0.0.
